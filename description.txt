Vanity Studio lets you draw your own clothing and accessories right inside the game.

Open the inventory and click the colour wheel button where the achievement advisor used to be (the little switch under it brings the advisor back).

- Paint head, body, leg, front and back pieces on a vanilla-sized canvas, with layers, opacity and a timeline for animated outfits
- Every pose the character can be in (standing, walking, jumping, flying, swinging) is previewed live, and any pose can get its own drawing
- Copy the sprite of armor you're wearing and repaint it: stats stay exactly the same, only the look changes
- Designs are saved outside your character and world, so every character on every world and server can wear them
- Other players on the server see what you wear, and see your changes while you draw
- Export to a vanilla-layout sprite sheet, or share .vsd files with friends
